# Product Requirements Document

## 1. Summary

Build an Android application for a single owner managing one universal local business. The app must work without internet, store data in local SQLite, provide simple cash-basis finance tracking, and preserve an audit-ready journal for edits and corrections. It must support multiple cash/bank/mobile-wallet accounts, customer receivables, supplier payables, optional attachments, local backups, Bangla/English, BDT, and light/dark/system themes. No final app name is defined.

## 2. Goals and success measures

### Goals

- Record a normal transaction in seconds through a Smart Form.
- Show trustworthy cash position, cash-basis profit, receivables, and payables.
- Preserve financial history while allowing corrections.
- Make the product useful with no account, cloud, or network connection.
- Make backup, restore, security, and exports understandable to a non-accountant.

### Success measures

- New user completes onboarding with one account and opening balance without assistance.
- A standard paid transaction can be entered and confirmed in under 30 seconds after setup.
- Every posted journal is balanced; derived balances reconcile after edits, deletes, backup restore, and app restart.
- Seven automatic backups and all manual exports are discoverable and restorable.
- Core flows pass Bangla and English accessibility review.

## 3. Scope and non-goals

### In scope

Single business/owner, cash-basis accounting, accounts, contacts, receivables/payables, Smart Entry, partial payments, transfers, recurring transactions, due and recurring-bill reminders, audit-preserving edits, unlimited reports, PDF/CSV, attachments, PIN/biometric, local backups, localization and themes.

### Non-goals

Cloud sync, multi-user or multi-business, inventory/POS, payroll, tax filing, payment processing, online banking feeds, and remote server analytics.

## 4. Personas and assumptions

Primary user is a local-business owner with limited accounting training and one Android device. The owner may use cash, bank, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, or another account. The device may be offline for long periods. Data is private and the owner is the only actor.

## 5. Core user flows

### First run

1. Choose Bangla or English.
2. Create owner and business profile.
3. Choose BDT/format preferences.
4. Add at least one financial account.
5. Enter a dated opening balance (zero accepted) and confirm opening equity entry.
6. Review default categories.
7. Set PIN and optionally enable biometric unlock.
8. Enable/configure daily backup and reach dashboard.

### Record a cash sale/expense

1. Tap Add Transaction.
2. Select income/expense; enter amount; choose suggested category and account.
3. Optionally expand advanced fields and attach proof.
4. Validate, preview effect, and save.
5. Post balanced journal, update derived balances, show confirmation.

### Record credit and settle later

Create credit income/expense with customer/supplier and due amount. Do not count it in cash P&L. Later choose Receive Due/Pay Due, select contact/account, enter full or partial amount, and post the settlement; recognize only the settled amount in cash reporting.

### Correct a transaction

Open transaction, edit fields, optionally provide reason, confirm impact. Preserve original, write audit record, post reversal/replacement or adjustment entries, and refresh all affected reports.

### Backup and restore

Automatic backup runs daily when enabled and conditions permit. User can create a manual export at any time. Restore lists validated backups, warns that current data will be replaced, requires PIN confirmation, restores database plus attachments, runs reconciliation, and records the event.

## 6. Functional requirements

### FR-01 Onboarding

The system shall block completion until owner, business, at least one account, and opening balance are valid. It shall support zero opening balance and record opening date. Opening balance shall not appear as income.

### FR-02 Transaction entry

The system shall provide basic and advanced fields, dynamic type-specific fields, optional attachments, partial payments, remembered last-used account, recent categories, real-time validation, duplicate warning, and unsaved-change warning. It shall save offline.

### FR-03 Contacts and settlements

The system shall maintain customer/supplier sub-ledgers, statements, balances, due dates, partial settlement, and overpayment validation.

### FR-04 Accounts

The system shall support multiple account types, account statements, archive instead of delete when history exists, and transfers that do not affect P&L.

### FR-05 Recurrence and reminders

The system shall support automatic and manual recurring transactions, configurable schedules, next-run preview, idempotent run tracking, due reminders, and recurring bill reminders. Notifications must work locally and support snooze/dismiss/mark-paid.

### FR-06 Accounting and journal

Every posted event shall produce a balanced journal. Journal entries and lines are authoritative; dashboard caches are rebuildable. The system shall support cash income/expense, credit events, settlements, transfers, owner capital/withdrawal, loans, refunds, adjustments, and opening balances.

### FR-07 Editing and deletion

Always allow edits subject to validation. Never overwrite posted financial history. Delete shall create a reversal/logical-deletion event. Capture before/after values, timestamp, action, and optional reason.

### FR-08 Reports and exports

Provide unlimited custom date ranges and presets, comparisons, cash P&L, cashflow, account, transaction, category, contact, receivable, and payable reports. Export PDF and CSV with localized formatting and stable totals.

### FR-09 Backup/restore

Run daily automatic local backups; retain seven automatic generations; retain manual exports separately. Include attachments, metadata, checksum, schema version, and restore validation.

### FR-10 Security/settings

Require PIN, support biometric, lock on launch/background timeout, rate-limit attempts, protect keys with Android Keystore where available, and provide language/theme/format/notification preferences.

## 7. Data model

SQLite tables (names illustrative): `business_profile`, `owner_profile`, `accounts`, `opening_balances`, `chart_of_accounts`, `categories`, `contacts`, `transactions`, `transaction_allocations`, `journal_entries`, `journal_lines`, `payments`, `ledger_entries`, `attachments`, `recurring_templates`, `recurring_runs`, `reminders`, `app_settings`, `backup_history`, `audit_logs`.

Key constraints: UUID primary keys; created/updated timestamps; soft-delete/archive flags; integer minor units or fixed decimal for money; ISO/local date separation where needed; foreign keys; unique idempotency key for recurring runs; immutable journal rows except compensating entries.

## 8. Accounting rules

1. Journal debits equal credits for every posted entry.
2. Cash income is recognized when money is received; cash expense when money is paid.
3. Credit sale creates receivable, not cash income; customer settlement recognizes cash income.
4. Credit purchase creates payable, not cash expense; supplier settlement recognizes cash expense.
5. Partial settlement recognizes only the paid/received amount and reduces the open balance.
6. Transfers change account balances only.
7. Owner investment and loans increase cash/equity or liability, not income.
8. Owner withdrawal and loan principal repayment are not operating expense; loan interest is expense.
9. Refunds/adjustments reverse or correct the original classification.
10. Opening balance posts to opening equity/capital, never income.
11. Current balances and reports derive from journal lines; cached summaries must be rebuildable.

## 9. Validation and edge cases

- Amount must be positive, within configured precision, and within safe storage limits.
- Account, category, and counterparty must match transaction type.
- Payment cannot exceed outstanding due unless explicit overpayment handling is selected.
- Dates may be historical; reporting is unlimited. Future-dated recurring instances remain pending until due.
- Archived accounts cannot receive new transactions.
- Duplicate recurring runs are prevented by template/date idempotency key.
- Failed notification or backup must not lose a transaction; show retry status.
- Attachment missing/corrupt path is flagged without invalidating the journal.
- Restore must reject bad checksum/schema and leave current data untouched on failure.
- PIN lockout is temporary and recoverable through the documented local reset flow.
- Rounding is deterministic and consistent across journal, dashboard, PDF, and CSV.
- Empty reports show zero-state explanations, not errors.

## 10. Non-functional requirements

- Offline-first: all core reads/writes work without network.
- Integrity: foreign keys, transactions/atomic commits, balanced-journal check, and post-restore reconciliation.
- Performance: common dashboard and entry interactions target <300 ms on supported mid-range devices; paginated lists and indexed dates/accounts/contacts.
- Reliability: crash-safe writes, retryable backup, and no silent data loss.
- Privacy/security: no cloud transmission; encrypted/protected local secrets; explicit sharing.
- Accessibility/localization: Bangla and English strings externalized; TalkBack labels, contrast, scalable text, and touch targets.
- Maintainability: versioned migrations, repository/service separation, deterministic accounting engine, testable report queries.

## 11. Acceptance criteria

- Fresh install cannot reach dashboard without a valid business, account, and opening balance.
- Cash sale/expense changes the selected account and cash P&L correctly.
- Credit sale/purchase changes receivable/payable only; settlement changes cash P&L by settled amount.
- Transfer changes source/destination balances and leaves P&L unchanged.
- Edit and delete preserve the original event, produce compensating entries, and appear in audit history.
- All journal entries balance; reconciliation catches and reports corruption.
- Unlimited date filters and all listed reports return correct totals and export to PDF/CSV.
- Seven automatic backups rotate correctly; manual exports remain retained; restore includes attachments.
- Recurring templates create at most one run per scheduled occurrence; reminders can be snoozed/dismissed/marked paid.
- PIN/biometric lock, Bangla/English, and light/dark/system settings work after restart and offline.

## 12. Release phases

### Phase 1 — Foundation

Onboarding, single business, mandatory opening balance, accounts, categories, SQLite migrations, journal engine, PIN/biometric, core dashboard.

### Phase 2 — Daily operations

Smart Entry, contacts, receivable/payable, partial payments, transfers, attachments, editing, logical deletion, audit log.

### Phase 3 — Insight and continuity

Reports, unlimited ranges, comparisons, PDF/CSV, daily backups, seven-generation rotation, manual exports, restore/reconciliation, localization/themes/accessibility.

### Phase 4 — Automation and hardening

Automatic/manual recurring transactions, due and recurring-bill reminders, notification controls, performance tuning, migration testing, failure recovery, and release readiness.

## 13. Open decisions for implementation

Choose final app name, minimum Android SDK/device support, exact attachment size limits, default notification times, PIN recovery UX, and whether automatic recurring items are posted immediately or require a pending confirmation. These decisions must not change the locked accounting, privacy, offline, or audit requirements above.
